Current Vacancy: Senior Educators

Tenzin Academy is looking for passionate, experienced, and highly motivated teachers to join our faculty for the upcoming academic session. We value deep subject knowledge and a commitment to nurturing the next generation of Bhutanese leaders.

Required Qualifications

  • Master's or Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject.
  • Post Graduate Diploma in Education (PGDE) or equivalent.
  • Minimum of 10+ years of teaching experience in reputable schools.
  • Proven track record of excellence in board examination results.
  • Commitment to GNH values and holistic education.

Subjects Needed

Physics Chemistry Mathematics English Dzongkha Economics Accountancy

Benefits

  • Competitive salary package commensurate with experience.
  • Subsidized housing/hostel facility.
  • Professional development opportunities.
  • A supportive and dynamic working environment.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit their CV, academic transcripts, and a cover letter explaining their teaching philosophy to:

tenzinacademy@education.gov.bt

Or contact us at +975-17578882 / 17805484 for more details.

Deadline for application: November 30, 2025
